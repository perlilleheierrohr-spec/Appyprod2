Questions

Is the camera recording me?

No. Each camera frame is analysed on your iPhone to find the position of your joints and then thrown away. No video or photo is ever saved or sent anywhere.

Do I need an account?

No. RepCam works without sign-up. Your Pro subscription is tied to your Apple ID, so it follows you to a new iPhone. Just tap Restore purchases.

Where do I put the phone?

On the floor, leaning against a wall or a shoe, about two to three metres away. Before each exercise RepCam shows a picture of where to stand.

Which iPhones are supported?

Any iPhone that runs the latest iOS. RepCam uses the front camera.

How do I cancel Pro?

Open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions and RepCam. You can also tap Manage subscription in RepCam's settings.

The count is off. What can I do?

Most miscounts come from the phone position. Tap the question mark during a set to see exactly where to put your phone, and make sure your whole body is in the picture. Still stuck? Contact support.
